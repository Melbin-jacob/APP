# Launch compliance: common traps

How GetTradePros handles six issues that often catch new web apps. Each row says what the
code does and what the operator still has to do. Not legal advice: have counsel review before launch.

| # | Risk | Law | Status in the code | Operator action |
|---|------|-----|--------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | A child under 13 signs up | COPPA, 16 CFR § 312 | **Handled.** Accounts are 18+ (Terms § 2). Sign-up asks a neutral month and year of birth (no hint of the cut-off, nothing pre-selected); under-18s get no account and nothing is stored; a 24 h cookie blocks an immediate retry. Google / email-link sign-ups must give a birth date on the accept-terms page; an under-age account is deleted on the spot. Only the date age was confirmed is kept (`User.ageConfirmedAt`), never the birth date. Privacy Policy has a "Children" section. | None. If the site is ever aimed at children, COPPA consent rules apply instead. |
| 2 | Third-party resources send visitors' IP addresses before consent | LG München, 3 O 17493/20 (Jan 2022); GDPR | **Handled.** Fonts are self-hosted (Montserrat via `next/font/local`, system fonts otherwise). No Google Fonts, analytics, maps or CDNs on public pages. Photos businesses link from other sites are copied to our storage when saved; outside banner images are proxied by our server; the newsletter background is local. The browser policy (CSP `img-src 'self' data: blob:`, `script-src` nonce) blocks any outside image or script that slips in. Ad scripts load only after advertising consent, in a sandboxed iframe. Address suggestions are looked up server-side. | Keep it that way: add new third-party services server-side, or behind consent. |
| 3 | Session replay records keystrokes | California Invasion of Privacy Act (wiretapping), § 631 | **Handled.** No session replay, heatmap or keystroke analytics. Visitor counting is cookieless, server-side daily totals only. The CSP blocks third-party scripts, so a replay tool can't be added by accident. | Don't add session replay. If you ever do: consent first, mask all inputs, and update the Privacy Policy. |
| 4 | Emails without unsubscribe link or postal address | CAN-SPAM (15 U.S.C. § 7704); CASL (Canada) | **Handled.** Every email ends with the company name and mailing address. Review requests carry a one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) and honour opt-outs. Newsletter sign-up is double opt-in. `sendEmail({ commercial: true })` refuses to send without an unsubscribe link, so a launch email can't go out without one. Production refuses to start without `LEGAL_ADDRESS`. | Set `LEGAL_ADDRESS` and `LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME`. Send any newsletter or launch email through `sendEmail` with `commercial: true` and an unsubscribe link, or through a provider that adds both. |
| 5 | Subscription renewal terms not disclosed | Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 17600-17606 (Automatic Renewal Law); FTC Negative Option Rule | **Not applicable today:** nothing is sold and there are no paid plans. | Before adding paid listings or plans: show the price, billing frequency and that it renews until cancelled next to the buy button; get express consent (unticked checkbox); email an acknowledgement with cancellation steps; offer online cancellation as easy as sign-up; send reminders before renewals and free-trial conversions. |
| 6 | User uploads without a registered DMCA agent | 17 U.S.C. § 512(c) | **Handled in the code.** `/legal/copyright` names the designated agent, explains notices, counter-notices and the repeat-infringer policy; Terms §§ 6 and 9 refer to it; footer links to it. Admins can remove a business logo (audited) and remove reviews; repeat infringers' accounts can be suspended. Production logs a warning until `DMCA_AGENT_NAME` is set. | **Register a designated agent** at https://dmca.copyright.gov ($6, renew every 3 years), then set `DMCA_AGENT_NAME`, `COPYRIGHT_EMAIL`, `DMCA_AGENT_ADDRESS` (and optionally `DMCA_AGENT_PHONE`) to match. Handle notices within a few days; keep a log. |
